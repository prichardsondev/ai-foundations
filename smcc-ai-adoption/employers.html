Employer Interview Kit

AI and entry-level work

Purpose

Use this kit to gather local industry input on how AI is changing entry-level work, what human skills matter more because of AI, and what AI-assisted work habits SMCC graduates should practice. Employer input should help SMCC build discipline-specific examples, testimonials, assignments, and training materials.

Who To Contact

  • Health systems, clinics, labs, EMS/fire partners.
  • BIW, manufacturing, machining, welding, robotics, marine design, construction, HVAC/plumbing/electrical employers.
  • Automotive and marine service employers.
  • Banks, insurance, hospitality, restaurants, tourism, customer service, and small businesses.
  • IT, cybersecurity, managed service providers, cloud support, and software employers.
  • Schools, childcare providers, behavioral health, and human services organizations.
  • Municipal, facilities, public works, and operations partners.

Outreach Email

Subject: Quick SMCC request: how AI is changing entry-level work in your field

Hello [Name],

SMCC is developing practical AI adoption training for faculty and staff so our graduates are prepared for the workplaces they are entering without losing the human judgment, communication, technical skill, and professionalism your field depends on.

Would you be willing to share a short testimonial or join a 20-minute interview about how AI is changing entry-level work in your organization or industry? We are especially interested in what new hires should know, what misuse concerns you, and what human skills matter even more because of AI.

We can use your input as a written quote, short video/audio clip, or internal curriculum note, depending on your preference and consent.

Thank you for helping us keep SMCC training grounded in real Maine workforce needs.

Best,
Paul

Consent Language

  • May SMCC use your name, title, organization, and comments?
  • Do you prefer public attribution, anonymous summary, or internal-only use?
  • May SMCC record audio or video for note-taking or testimonial use?
  • Do you want to review any quote or clip before it is used?
  • This is not a vendor endorsement and should not include proprietary workflows or confidential client, patient, student, employee, or partner data.

Final consent/release language pending SMCC review before external publication or public testimonial use.

Interview Script

Thank you for speaking with us. SMCC is building practical AI training for faculty and staff so students get clearer, field-specific preparation. We are not asking you to endorse a vendor or reveal confidential information. We are trying to understand what AI is changing in entry-level work, what misuse worries employers, and what habits would make graduates safer and more useful on day one.

Interview Questions

  1. How is AI already changing work in your field?
  2. Which entry-level tasks are becoming AI-assisted?
  3. What AI skills or habits would make a new hire more useful?
  4. What AI misuse worries you most?
  5. What human skills matter more because of AI?
  6. What would make an SMCC graduate stand out?
  7. Are there examples of AI use that are unsafe, unethical, or unacceptable in your workplace?
  8. Are entry-level employees producing AI-generated work that is difficult for senior staff to review?
  9. What makes AI-assisted work trustworthy in your field?
  10. What AI-assisted work habits would make a new hire safer and more useful on day one?
  11. Do you want graduates trained in prompt writing, verification, documentation, review discipline, or all of the above?
  12. What should students still be able to do without AI?
  13. Would you be willing to review a sample assignment or classroom scenario?
  14. May SMCC quote you in training materials, and if so in what format?

Optional Probes

  • Can you describe an AI-assisted work product that was easy or hard to review?
  • What evidence, documentation, or human explanation would make that work more trustworthy?
  • Which habits would you want SMCC students to practice before entering your workplace?

Testimonial Formats

  • Written quote.
  • Short video.
  • Audio clip.
  • Internal curriculum note.
  • Anonymous summary.

Follow-Up Workflow

  1. Save interview notes with consent preference clearly marked.
  2. Pull out field-specific AI tasks, risks, and human skills.
  3. Identify any quote or example that needs employer review before use.
  4. Map feedback to SMCC pathways or programs.
  5. Share relevant findings with faculty/staff owners for review.
  6. Add employer-informed examples only after owner review.

Employer examples should be verified and reviewed before being treated as evidence or added to training materials.
